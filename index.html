Our Story & Purpose

About the ZAY Youth Fundraiser

The ZAY (Zomi Adventist Youth) fundraiser is proudly organized by the energetic youth group of Zomi SDA Church in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

Our mission is to nurture strong Christian character, teamwork, practical work ethic, and servant leadership among young people. The money raised through lawn mowing and our home-style food sales directly supports:

Youth Bible Camps & Retreats

Providing camp fees & transport for spiritual growth and team building.

Community Outreach & Food Drives

Distributing groceries and assisting elderly church and neighborhood members.

Youth Ministry & Music Programs

Equipping our youth choir, audio-visual team, and Sabbath school classrooms.

Education & Mission Trips

Scholarship aids and youth mission endeavors across the United States.
